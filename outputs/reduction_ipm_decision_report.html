19	## Principle
20	
21	The FEL's per-shot incident flux `I0` fluctuates ~100 % shot-to-shot and drifts over

23	divided by its own flux before combining:

28	S(q) = Σ_i f_i(q) / Σ_i n_i          # ratio-of-sums form  (robust to monitor noise)
29	```
